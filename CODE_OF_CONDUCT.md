# Code of conduct

We want Agent Loop to be a welcoming project for people with different
backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives. Treat others with respect, discuss
technical disagreements in good faith, and keep feedback focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this standard, contact
[@jpshelley](https://github.com/jpshelley) privately. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical. The maintainer may remove content, restrict project
participation, or take other action needed to protect the community.
